## A0 Level Test

After the learning path, the reusable LevelAssessment engine checks listening, vocabulary/meaning, sentence structure, reading, optional speaking practice, and writing.

## Planned level ladder

### A1 — Everyday English
Family, possessions, routines, Present Continuous, practical questions, everyday interactions, 100–150 word production.

### A2 — Stories & Life
Past Simple, Past Continuous, future forms, travel/work/school/health vocabulary, short storytelling, 150–250 word writing.

### B1 — Connected English
Tense system in depth, clauses, connected ideas, paragraph control, 300–400 word organized writing.

### B2 — Strong English
Advanced grammar, fluent discussion, argument basics, 500–750 word essays.

### C1 — Academic & College English
Academic reading, rhetoric, evidence, source integration, analysis, research writing, 1,000+ word papers.

### C2 — Advanced Natural English
Nuance, register, rhetoric, sophisticated speaking, professional and academic stylistic control.
